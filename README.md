# No-Easy-Fix
A Mod for HBS BattleTech / BEXT that make repairs and refits more in line with canon and limits the ability to reconfigure non-OmniMechs..

No Easy Fix treats a standard BattleMech as a finished machine. You can still repair it, change its armor, and swap gear that fits the space the stock loadout already used — but you cannot hang extra weapons or equipment in leftover empty slots. That leftover space is for OmniMechs. Changing a location to a different kind of weapon or gear is an expensive refit; repairs and like-for-like swaps stay at normal cost.

This makes 'Mech repair much harder, especially in the early game, and is deigned to give more of a canon feel to 'Mech repair and refit. Saved refitted 'Mechs, and those refitted and sent to storage retain their final space configuration and are not reverted to a stock layout.

Overview

HBS BattleTech lets you fill every empty inventory box a chassis has, if you can, so a “stock” 'Mech can grow a lot of extra guns once you own it. No Easy Fix puts the factory layout back in charge for ordinary BattleMechs, and leaves OmniMechs as the machines that can still use leftover space.

On a standard BattleMech, each location may only use as much inventory space as that variant already used in its published stock loadout. Though leftover empty boxes still appear on the silhouette they are locked and cannot have equipment placed in them. You may pull a weapon and put something else in that location if it fits the same budget (i.e. an AC/10 for an AC/20). You cannot add a small laser to an unused support hardpoint, hang gear on an empty arm, or stuff extra heat sinks into space the stock kit never used.

You can still repair components, structure, and armor at normal prices; strip and rebuild within the stock budget; swap like for like (any Medium Laser for a Medium Laser, ammo for the same ammo); and fit a smaller item into a larger stock space, with the unused remainder staying locked to that wepaon/equipment type. Heat banks and similar heat gear can use locations that already had heat sinks, if they fit. Targeting computers, TTS, C3, ECM, and other electronics/mods are not treated as extra guns in empty structure.

You cannot add extra weapons, ammo, jump jets, or heat sinks into leftover empty slots, or turn an unused hardpoint into a new mount unless that location still has unused stock budget.

OmniMechs are unchanged by the lock: leftover space still works, and Omni-style installs stay cheap.

Refit cost: standard repair costs remain the same, and same-kind swaps stay at the usual install price (i.e. a Medoum Laser++ for a Medium Laser). Installing a different kind of thing in that location is a type-change refit and costs more C-bills and shop time (i.e. a Medium Laser in a Medium Pulse Laser locations). Moving gear is charged as an install at the destination.

The MechLab provides tinted stock ghosts showing where different weapong types fit on the chassis, while a short bar shows locked leftover tiles, as well as a pointer toll that shows how much of the stock budget is in use. F8 fades the live kit so you can read the factory layout.

Existing customized ’Mechs in your save games keep what they have and cannot grow further. New machines use the published stock layout. The mod does not turn Omnis into free-form pod ’Mechs, rewrite chassis files, or fill empty crits with dummy equipment.
